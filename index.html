1. Who we are

Lomi Broker is a real-estate marketplace for buying, selling, renting, and listing properties in Ethiopia. The Android application package name is com.lomibroker.broker.

When this policy says “we”, “us”, or “Lomi Broker”, it means the operator of the Lomi Broker app and the website at broker.lomi-school.com.

2. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the service. Depending on how you use Lomi Broker, this may include:

Account and profile

  • Mobile phone number, used to create and verify your account with an SMS one-time code
  • Name, email address, profile photo, and other details you enter in your profile
  • If you sign in with Google or Apple, the name, email, and identifier those services share with us after you approve

Listings, chat, and files

  • Property details you publish (title, price, address, description, photos, videos, and related documents)
  • Messages you send to other users or to support through in-app chat
  • Favourites, saved searches, enquiries, and reports you submit

Location

  • Approximate or precise location, if you allow it, so we can show nearby properties, a map pin, and distance
  • Addresses and map coordinates you type or drop on a listing

Device and app activity

  • Device type, operating system, app version, language, and unique app/install identifiers
  • Push-notification token so we can send listing and chat alerts
  • Crash logs, performance data, and basic diagnostics
  • IP address and timestamps when you connect to our servers

Payments and subscriptions

  • Package or subscription you choose, purchase status, and related order identifiers
  • We do not store your full card number. Card and Play Store payments are handled by the payment provider you use

3. How we use information

  • Create and secure your account, including sending SMS verification codes
  • Show, search, filter, and map properties
  • Let you publish listings, chat with other users, and manage subscriptions
  • Process in-app purchases and confirm that a package was paid
  • Send service messages, such as OTP, listing status, chat, and security alerts
  • Improve reliability, prevent fraud and abuse, and comply with Ethiopian law
  • Show ads in the app if advertising is enabled in our settings (for example Google AdMob)

We do not sell your personal information.

4. How we share information

We share information only with:

  • Other users — public profile and listing content you choose to publish
  • Service providers who process data for us, including:
    • Google (Play, Firebase, Maps, sign-in, Cloud Messaging, and ads if enabled)
    • Apple (Sign in with Apple, if you use it)
    • SMS delivery partners in Ethiopia, currently Geez SMS, to send verification codes to your phone number
    • Payment processors such as Google Play Billing and, if enabled, Stripe, Razorpay, or similar gateways
    • Hosting providers that run broker.lomi-school.com
  • Authorities when required by law or to protect users, property, or the service

Each provider has its own privacy terms. Google’s policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Location

Location is used to find nearby properties, place a listing on the map, and calculate distance. You can deny or later revoke location permission in Android settings. Some map and “near me” features will not work without it. We do not continuously track your movement in the background for advertising.

6. Payments

Paid packages and subscriptions may be billed through Google Play or another payment method shown in the app. Google processes Play purchases under its own terms. We receive confirmation that a purchase succeeded (for example product ID and purchase token) so we can unlock the package on your Lomi Broker account. Refunds for Play purchases follow Google Play’s refund rules.

7. How long we keep information

We keep account, listing, and transaction records for as long as your account is active and as long as we need them to provide the service, resolve disputes, and meet legal, tax, or security requirements. You may ask us to delete your account. Some records may remain in backups for a limited period, or where the law requires us to keep them.

8. Security

We use HTTPS, account authentication (including SMS OTP), and access controls on our servers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Please keep your phone and OTP codes private. We will never ask you to share an OTP by chat or email.

9. Children

Lomi Broker is for adults who can enter property and payment transactions. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights and choices

  • Update your profile inside the app
  • Turn off location, camera, photos, microphone, or notifications in Android settings
  • Sign out or ask us to delete your account and associated personal data
  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you

To request access or deletion, use the contact details below and write from the phone number or email on your account so we can verify it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, the law, or our providers change. The “Last updated” date at the top will change. The current version will always be published at this page. Continued use of Lomi Broker after an update means you accept the revised policy.
