Lomi Broker · Account deletion

Delete your Lomi Broker account

This page is for users of the Lomi Broker Android app. Follow the steps below to delete your account, or request deletion here if you no longer have the app installed.

Developer: Lomi Broker · Package: com.lomibroker.broker · Last updated: 8 November 2026

How to delete your account in the Lomi Broker app

This is the fastest way. You must be signed in.

  1. Open the Lomi Broker app on your Android phone.
  2. Sign in with the same phone number, email, Google, or Apple account you used to register.
  3. Open Profile (your account screen).
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Read the confirmation message, then tap Delete.

When this succeeds, your Lomi Broker account is closed and you are signed out. You can create a new account later with the same phone number or email if you wish.

Request deletion without the app

If you uninstalled Lomi Broker, lost access to the device, or the in-app option does not work, request deletion from this page.

Send a deletion request to Lomi Broker through our website:

Open broker.lomi-school.com

In your message, include all of the following:

  • Subject or first line: Lomi Broker account deletion request
  • Your full name
  • The phone number and/or email on the account
  • A statement that you want the account and associated personal data deleted

We only process requests that we can match to an existing Lomi Broker account.

What data is deleted

After we complete deletion, we remove or unlink:

  • Your account profile (name, phone number, email, photo, login identifiers)
  • Saved favourites, searches, and in-app preferences tied to your user ID
  • Your property listings and the photos/files you uploaded with them
  • Chat threads and enquiry history linked to your account
  • App session tokens so you can no longer sign in

What may be kept, and for how long

  • Payment records: Google Play (and any other payment provider) keep their own purchase history. We may keep a non-personal record of a package purchase (date, product ID, Play order token) for accounting, refunds, and fraud prevention for up to 24 months, or longer if the law requires.
  • Security and server logs: IP address, device, and error logs may remain for up to 90 days, then they are overwritten.
  • Backups: Encrypted backups may still contain a copy of deleted data for up to 30 days until the backup cycle replaces them. They are not used to restore your account.
  • Legal holds: If we must keep information for a dispute, fraud investigation, or Ethiopian legal obligation, we keep only what the law requires and delete it when that purpose ends.
  • Content other people still need: Messages another user already received may remain in their inbox, without your live account attached.

We do not charge a fee to delete an account. Deletion cannot be undone.

How long deletion takes

  • In the app: usually immediately when you confirm Delete account.
  • From this webpage: we aim to complete the request within 7 days after we verify that the request belongs to you.
